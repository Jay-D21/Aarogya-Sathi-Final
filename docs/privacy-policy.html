Overview

Aarogya Sathi (आरोग्य साथी) is a personal AI health companion for India. This policy explains exactly what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

Your health data is sensitive. We treat it that way — most of it never leaves your device.

Health Connect Access

Aarogya Sathi uses Android Health Connect with read-only permissions. We:

  • Never write to or modify your Health Connect data
  • Never store Health Connect data on external servers
  • Never sell or share your health data for advertising or marketing
  • Read health data only while you are actively using the app

Third-Party Services

We work with the following services. No others.

Service Data Shared Purpose
Groq / SambaNova / Cerebras Chat text + anonymized health context AI response generation
WAQI API City name only Real-time air quality data
Open-Meteo Approximate GPS coordinates Weather (temperature, humidity)

We do not share your data with advertising networks, data brokers, or analytics platforms.

Data Security

  • On-device storage — Health profile, documents, and preferences stored locally using Android SharedPreferences and sandboxed file storage
  • Encrypted in transit — All server communication uses HTTPS/TLS
  • No persistent server logs — Chat sessions are processed in memory and discarded
  • No account required — The app works without personal identification

Your Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023:

  • Access — View all data stored by the app in your device settings
  • Correction — Edit your health profile at any time within the app
  • Erasure — Use the in-app "Delete Account & Local Data" button, or uninstall the app
  • Withdraw consent — Revoke Health Connect permissions via Android Settings → Health Connect → Aarogya Sathi

Children's Privacy

Aarogya Sathi is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

⚠️ Medical Disclaimer — Aarogya Sathi provides general health awareness information only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. In a medical emergency, call 108 immediately.
