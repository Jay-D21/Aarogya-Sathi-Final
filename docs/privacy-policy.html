Overview

Aarogya Sathi (आरोग्य साथी) is an AI-powered personal health companion app built by students at SVKM's NMIMS Shirpur Campus. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

🔒 Privacy-First · DPDP Act Compliant

We are committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 of India.

Health Connect Data

📱 Read-Only Access

Aarogya Sathi accesses health data through Android Health Connect with read-only permissions. We:

  • Never write to or modify your Health Connect data
  • Never store your Health Connect data on external servers
  • Never sell or share your health data with third parties for advertising or marketing
  • Read health data only when you actively use the app to provide personalized health context to the AI assistant

Health Connect Data Types Used

  • Steps — Daily activity monitoring and step goal tracking
  • Heart Rate & Resting Heart Rate — Cardiovascular health monitoring with ICMR clinical threshold alerts
  • Blood Pressure — Hypertension classification (Normal, Elevated, Stage 1, Stage 2)
  • Blood Glucose — Diabetes management with fasting/postprandial classification
  • Oxygen Saturation (SpO2) — Low-oxygen proactive alerts
  • Sleep — Sleep quality assessment for holistic health
  • Weight — BMI calculation using Asian ICMR cutoffs
  • Body Temperature — Fever detection and monitoring
  • Active & Total Calories Burned — Daily energy expenditure tracking
  • Distance — Activity distance tracking

How We Use Your Data

  • AI Health Guidance — Your health profile and wearable metrics are used to personalize AI responses based on ICMR (Indian Council of Medical Research) clinical guidelines
  • Proactive Health Alerts — We analyze vital signs (heart rate, SpO2, blood pressure) against ICMR clinical thresholds to generate health observations
  • Environmental Health — Your city location is used to fetch real-time Air Quality Index (AQI) data to provide pollution-aware health guidance
  • Medical File Management — Documents are stored entirely on your device for your personal reference

Data Storage & Security

  • Local Storage — Health profile, medical documents, and preferences are stored on your device using SharedPreferences and sandboxed file storage
  • Transit Security — All data sent to our backend is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • No Persistent Server Storage — Chat sessions are processed in memory and not permanently stored on our servers. Session data is ephemeral
  • No User Accounts Required — The app works as a guest without requiring personal identification

Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access — View all data stored by the app (available in your device settings)
  • Correction — Edit your health profile at any time within the app
  • Erasure — Delete all app data by clearing app data in Android Settings or uninstalling the app
  • Withdraw Consent — Revoke Health Connect permissions at any time through Android Settings → Health Connect → Aarogya Sathi

Children's Privacy

Aarogya Sathi is not designed for or directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Medical Disclaimer

Aarogya Sathi provides general health awareness information only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of your physician or qualified health provider with any questions regarding a medical condition. In case of a medical emergency, call 108 immediately.
