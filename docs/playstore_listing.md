## Full Description

Aarogya Sathi (आरोग्य साथी) is your personal AI health companion designed for India. Get personalized health guidance powered by ICMR guidelines, real-time air quality alerts, and seamless wearable integration — all with your data staying private on your device.

🩺 AI Health Chat
Ask health questions in English, Hindi, or Marathi. Get evidence-based responses grounded in ICMR clinical guidelines — not generic internet advice. The AI understands your health profile, medications, and vitals for truly personalized guidance.

⌚ Wearable Integration
Connect with Fitbit, Google Fit, or Samsung Health via Android Health Connect. Your steps, heart rate, SpO2, sleep, blood pressure, and blood glucose sync automatically.

🌬️ Air Quality Monitoring
Real-time AQI data from the nearest CPCB monitoring station with health impact warnings. Get proactive alerts when air quality in your area becomes unhealthy.

📊 Health Dashboard
Track blood pressure, blood sugar, weight, and symptoms with ICMR-standard classifications. Visual trends and weekly consistency tracking help you stay on top of your health.

🔔 Smart Health Alerts
Proactive notifications for abnormal vitals (high BP, low blood sugar, irregular heart rate, low SpO2) and hazardous air quality — thresholds based on ICMR and CPCB standards.

📁 Medical File Vault
Store prescriptions, lab reports, and scan images securely on your device. Organized by category — never lose a medical document again.

🔒 Privacy First
Your health data stays on your device. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023. No cloud storage of personal health records.

🇮🇳 Made for India
Multilingual support (English, Hindi, Marathi), ICMR clinical thresholds, Asian BMI classifications, and CPCB air quality standards.

⚠️ Medical Disclaimer
Aarogya Sathi provides health information for educational purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider.
