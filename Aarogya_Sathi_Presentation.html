PROBLEM STATEMENT

The Crises of NCDs, Pollution & Language.

High Prevalence of NCDs

  • Non-Communicable Diseases (NCDs) like diabetes and CVDs account for ~63% of all deaths in India.
  • The sheer volume of undiagnosed conditions severely overburdens the healthcare system.

The "Pollution-Blind" Gap

  • Standard algorithms ignore environmental toxicity.
  • Over 200,000+ emergency respiratory cases are linked directly to poor air quality in cities like Delhi.

The Linguistic Divide

  • Over 90% of health apps are English-only.
  • Massive exclusion of the non-English speaking population from preventive AI healthcare.
03

MOTIVATION & NEED

From Personal Need to Macroeconomic Imperative.

Personal Inspiration

My father has high cholesterol and constantly has small, everyday questions about his diet and routine. We realized the profound need for a personalized, vernacular "Virtual Nurse" that is easy for him to use, answering daily queries without requiring a doctor's visit for every small doubt.

Macroeconomic Threat

NCDs project a $4.58 Trillion economic loss for India by 2030 (WEF). Preventative digital healthcare is no longer just a luxury; it is an absolute economic imperative to sustain national growth and workforce productivity.

04

PROJECT OBJECTIVES

Autonomous, Localized, Safe.

Agentic Triage

Deploy an AI "Virtual Nurse" to assess symptoms based strictly on ICMR guidelines, specifically constrained to avoid unverified medical diagnosis.

Hyper-Local Logic

Integrate live APIs (WAQI, OpenWeather) to dynamically alter health advice based on localized smog alerts and weather conditions.

Data Sovereignty

Architect a 100% Indian-hosted, DPDP Act (2023) compliant ecosystem where sensitive health records never cross national borders.

PROPOSED METHODOLOGY (2/2)

Agentic Workflow Execution.

Replacing static decision trees with autonomous, context-aware reasoning.

1. Intercept (Input)

Fetch live GPS and WAQI environmental data continuously in the background alongside user symptoms.

2. Retrieve (Processing)

Query ICMR guidelines via Vector DB to ensure the context retrieved is strictly medically grounded.

3. Reason (Analysis)

The Medical LLM correlates the reported symptoms directly with the retrieved context and smog alerts.

4. Filter (Output)

A strict heuristic block scans output to remove diagnostic language before delivering advice.

11

FEASIBILITY OF IMPLEMENTATION

Scaling Sovereign AI Practically.

Economic & Compute Arbitrage

High-parameter AI is cost-prohibitive. By utilizing domestic providers (E2E Networks) and 4-bit model quantization, we reduce H100 GPU inference costs by ~70% vs AWS/GCP, ensuring financial viability for real-world scaling.

Data Integration Viability

APIs for environmental data (WAQI) and foundational language translation (Bhashini) are public, mature, and documented. This bypasses the need to train complex models from scratch.

Legal Compliance

Zero-knowledge local inference ensures 100% data residency. The architecture inherently meets the strict consent and storage requirements of the DPDP Act (2023).

Technical Readiness

Flutter provides a single-codebase solution for iOS/Android, drastically reducing UI development overhead, while FastAPI handles asynchronous LLM streaming efficiently.
