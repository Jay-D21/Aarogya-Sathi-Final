02  /  Problem Statement

The Crisis

India's preventive healthcare is broken at three levels

🏥
63%
Deaths caused by NCDs
Non-Communicable Diseases (diabetes, CVDs, COPD) are India's #1 killer. Yet preventive infrastructure is near-zero for 1.4B people.

ICMR-INDIAB 2023: 26.4% urban diabetes prevalence · WEF: $4.58 Trillion projected economic loss by 2030

🌫️
4.2M
Premature deaths from PM2.5
South Asia bears the world's highest pollution burden. Delhi residents lose 11.9 years of life expectancy due to particulate matter alone.

AQLI 2024 · Lelieveld et al., Nature 2015 · 200K+ respiratory admissions linked to AQI events annually

🗣️
90%
Excluded from AI health tools
Over 90% of Indians don't speak English as their primary language. All major AI health platforms — Practo, Ada Health, WebMD — are English-only.

Census of India: 22 official languages · Health literacy gaps compound chronic disease outcomes

04  /  Objectives of the Project

Goals

Five measurable, bounded objectives

O1

Clinically-Safe AI Triage

Deploy a Digital Health Assistant that assesses user symptoms using strictly ICMR-grounded RAG, preventing hallucinated medical advice.

O2

Hyper-Local Environment Awareness

Integrate live WAQI pollution APIs to dynamically alter health advice based on real-time AQI — "stay indoors, high PM2.5 alert."

O3

Vernacular-First Delivery

Deliver health intelligence in 22 official Indian languages via Bhashini + IndicTrans2, enabling truly inclusive access.

O4

Data Sovereignty Architecture

Build a 100% Indian-hosted, DPDP Act (2023) compliant system where sensitive health data never crosses national borders.

O5

Validated Accuracy Benchmarking

Measure system accuracy against established medical AI benchmarks (MMed-RAG framework, CLINIC benchmark) before any user deployment.

05  /  Scope of the Project

Boundaries

Defining the MVP — what we build vs. what we exclude

✅ In Scope Technical Deliverables

📱Cross-platform mobile app (Flutter) with offline SQLite caching for rural connectivity
⚡FastAPI asynchronous microservices backend with PostgreSQL database
🧠ICMR-grounded RAG pipeline using BioMistral / Llama 3 + Pinecone vector DB
🌬️Real-time WAQI air quality API integration for contextual health alerts
🔤Vernacular NLP via Bhashini + IndicTrans2 (22 Indian languages)
📄OCR-based physical lab report parsing for symptom context
🔒DPDP Act (2023) compliant sovereign data on E2E Networks (Indian cloud)

❌ Out of Scope Excluded

🩺Medical diagnoses or drug prescriptions (regulatory & legal constraint)
⌚Hardware wearable integration (Apple Watch, Fitbit, etc.)
🏛️Live ABDM production deployment (sandbox/testing mode only)
🚑Acute emergency triage or real-time teleconsultation
🍎iOS production release in MVP phase (Android only)

📍 Target Users & Geography

Urban Indian adults (25–60) with chronic NCD conditions. Initial geo-focus: High-AQI metros — Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru.

09  /  Feasibility of Implementation

Viability Analysis

Viable across all four dimensions of feasibility

💻

Technical Feasibility

  • BioMistral 7B at 4-bit quantization runs on a single A100 GPU — available on E2E Networks India
  • IndicTrans2 (AI4Bharat) is publicly available, achieves state-of-art chrF scores for medical translation
  • Flutter single codebase drastically reduces frontend development overhead
  • WAQI & Bhashini APIs are public, stable, and well-documented — no training needed

70% GPU cost reduction on E2E vs. AWS/GCP for equivalent compute

💰

Economic Feasibility

  • All core models are open-source — zero licensing fees vs. proprietary alternatives
  • Preventive health delivers 14.3:1 ROI (Masters et al., JECH 2017) — strong business case
  • India's digital health market projected at $372B by 2030 (NITI Aayog)
₹0
LLM licensing cost
14.3×
ROI on prevention
⚖️

Legal & Regulatory Feasibility

  • Zero-knowledge local inference ensures 100% data residency within India
  • Architecture inherently meets DPDP Act (2023) explicit consent and storage mandates
  • ABDM sandbox integration available for testing without live patient data
  • Scope explicitly excludes prescriptions — not classified as medical device under CDSCO
📋

Operational Feasibility

  • 3-member team with complementary skills: AI/ML, Mobile Dev, Backend Engineering
  • All constraints (WAQI, Bhashini, E2E APIs) are documented and testable from Day 1
  • Offline-first SQLite design handles India's variable connectivity without architectural changes
  • 12-week timeline is achievable — tasks scoped to MVP deliverables only
