PROBLEM STATEMENT

NCDs + Pollution + Language Barrier.

63% Mortality

Non-Communicable Diseases (NCDs) cause 63% of all deaths in India.

4.2M Deaths

Premature deaths globally from PM2.5 air pollution (South Asia bears the brunt).

90% Excluded

Of India doesn't speak English as a primary language, failing to access AI health tools.

MOTIVATION AND NEED

Existing apps don't talk
to the most vulnerable.

Platforms like Practo and eSanjeevani focus on reactive care. AI symptom checkers lack India-specific guidelines (ICMR), hyper-local AQI awareness, and true vernacular capability.

No AQI Integration English-Centric Reactive, Not Preventive
04

OBJECTIVES OF THE PROJECT

Proactive. Localized. Safe.

Proactive NCD Management

Delay onset and severity through continuous health tracking and dietary advice.

Hyper-Local Alerts

Correlate real-time CPCB AQI data with personal health profiles (e.g., COPD).

Vernacular First

Provide medical intelligence in 22 official Indian languages using Indic NLP.

Clinically Safe AI

Eliminate LLM hallucinations using ICMR-grounded Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

Technical Constraints

Indic NLP models are now highly accurate (e.g. IndicTrans2). Costs are optimized by using open-source LLMs and local embedding models.

Legal & Compliance

Architected to comply with India's DPDP Act (2023). Uses explicit consent and processes high-risk health data securely.
