# Coronary Artery Disease — ICMR Management Guidelines

## Definition
Impairment of blood flow through coronary arteries, typically caused by atherosclerotic plaque accumulation. South Asians develop CAD a decade earlier than Western counterparts with greater severity and diffuse lesions.

## Diagnostic Criteria & Investigations
| Investigation | Key Diagnostic Findings |
|---------------|--------------------------|
| 12-Lead ECG | ST-elevation, ST-depression, T-wave inversion, or new Q waves |
| Cardiac Biomarkers | Elevated high-sensitivity Troponin I or T (above 99th percentile) |
| Lipid Profile | Elevated LDL-C (>100 mg/dL or >70 mg/dL in high risk), High ApoB/Lp(a) |
| 2D Echocardiography | Regional wall motion abnormalities (RWMA), reduced LVEF |

## Clinical Severity Classification
- **Chronic Coronary Syndrome (Stable Angina)**: Predictable chest tightness on exertion, relieved by rest.
- **Unstable Angina (ACS)**: Angina at rest or with minimal exertion, accelerating frequency.
- **NSTEMI**: Myocardial infarction without ST elevation; elevated cardiac enzymes.
- **STEMI**: Acute transmural ischemia with ST elevation requiring immediate revascularization.

## India-Specific Risk Factors & Lifestyle Modifications
- **Premature CAD Phenotype**: Strong genetic predisposition, high lipoprotein(a), low HDL, high triglycerides, and visceral fat.
- **Cardioprotective Diet**: Emphasize whole grains (jowar, bajra, brown rice), green sabzi, dal, and walnuts. Avoid vanaspati, ghee re-heating, trans-fats, and sugary sweets (mithai).
- **Atypical Symptoms**: Diabetic and female Indian patients often present with breathlessness, nausea, or epigastric discomfort instead of classic angina.
- **Medical Therapy**: Your doctor may consider statins, antiplatelet agents (aspirin), beta-blockers, or emergency coronary angiography.

## ⚠️ Emergency Protocol — Call 112 Immediately
- Persistent retrosternal chest pressure, heaviness, or burning lasting >10 minutes
- Pain radiating to the jaw, neck, back, or left arm accompanied by cold sweating (diaphoresis)
- Unexplained sudden breathlessness, dizziness, or collapse
