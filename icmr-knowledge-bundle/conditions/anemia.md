# Anemia — ICMR Management Guidelines

## Definition
A condition characterized by a reduced hemoglobin (Hb) concentration or red blood cell count, leading to decreased oxygen-carrying capacity. Nutritional iron deficiency is the leading cause in India.

## ICMR / WHO Diagnostic Criteria (Hemoglobin Cutoffs)
| Population Group | Normal Hb | Mild Anemia | Moderate Anemia | Severe Anemia |
|------------------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|---------------|
| Adult Men (≥15 yrs) | ≥13.0 g/dL | 11.0–12.9 g/dL | 8.0–10.9 g/dL | <8.0 g/dL |
| Non-Pregnant Women (≥15 yrs) | ≥12.0 g/dL | 11.0–11.9 g/dL | 8.0–10.9 g/dL | <8.0 g/dL |
| Pregnant Women | ≥11.0 g/dL | 10.0–10.9 g/dL | 7.0–9.9 g/dL | <7.0 g/dL |

## Etiological Classification in India
- **Iron Deficiency Anemia (IDA)**: Microcytic hypochromic; high prevalence among adolescent girls and mothers (targeted by Anemia Mukt Bharat).
- **Megaloblastic Anemia (Vitamin B12 & Folate)**: Common in Indian vegetarian diets due to lack of animal sources; causes macrocytosis and fatigue.
- **Hemoglobinopathies**: Sickle cell trait/anemia and beta-thalassemia in specific tribal and geographical belts.

## India-Specific Dietary Guidance
- **Iron-Rich Indian Foods**: Green leafy vegetables (palak, methi, moringa leaves), jaggery (gur), black dates (khajoor), roasted chana, and horse gram (kulthi dal).
- **Bioavailability Enhancers**: Pair plant iron with Vitamin C (lemon squeezed over dal, amla, guava); avoid drinking tea or chai within 1 hour of meals as tannins block iron absorption.
- **Medical Therapy**: Your doctor may consider elemental iron and folic acid supplements (IFA) or injectable iron/vitamin B12 injections for severe deficits.

## ⚠️ Emergency Signs — Call 112 Immediately
- Chest pain, severe resting shortness of breath, or cardiac palpitations
- Sudden fainting (syncope), profound dizziness, or loss of consciousness
- Uncontrolled bleeding or active hematemesis/melena
