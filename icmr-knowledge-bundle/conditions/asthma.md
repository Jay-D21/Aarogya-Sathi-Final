# Asthma — ICMR Management Guidelines

## Definition
Asthma is a chronic inflammatory disease of the airways causing recurrent wheezing, breathlessness, chest tightness, and coughing.

## ICMR Diagnostic Criteria
- FEV1/FVC ratio < 0.7 post-bronchodilator
- Reversible airway obstruction: ≥12% and ≥200 mL improvement in FEV1 after bronchodilator

## Severity Classification (Indian GINA Adaptation)
| Severity | Frequency of symptoms | Nighttime symptoms | Lung function |
|----------|----------------------|-------------------|---------------|
| Intermittent | <2 days/week | ≤2/month | Normal |
| Mild Persistent | >2 days/week | 3-4/month | FEV1 ≥80% |
| Moderate Persistent | Daily | >1/week | FEV1 60-80% |
| Severe Persistent | Continuous | Frequent | FEV1 <60% |

## Environmental Triggers (India-Specific)
- **AQI > 150**: High risk for asthma attacks — avoid outdoor activity, use N95 mask
- **Biomass burning**: Common in rural areas — a major trigger
- **Monsoon**: Fungal spores and dust mites spike → more attacks
- **Winter fog**: PM2.5 and NO2 levels rise — peak risk season (Oct-Feb)

## Immediate Management (Non-Emergency)
- Use reliever inhaler (Salbutamol) at first sign of wheezing — discuss correct technique with doctor
- Stay indoors when AQI > 150
- Keep home dust-free; avoid carpets, heavy curtains

## ⚠️ Emergency Signs — Call 112 Immediately
- Severe breathlessness (cannot speak full sentences)
- Blue lips or fingernails (cyanosis)
- No relief after 3 puffs of reliever inhaler
- Peak flow < 50% of personal best

## Lifestyle Recommendations
- Breathing exercises (Pranayama) can help — with doctor guidance
- Avoid cold drinks, especially ice water
- Wear a scarf over nose and mouth in cold/dusty conditions
