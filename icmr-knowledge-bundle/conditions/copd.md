# COPD — ICMR Management Guidelines

## Definition
A progressive, non-reversible lung disorder marked by airflow limitation and persistent respiratory symptoms (dyspnea, chronic cough, sputum production).

## Diagnostic Criteria (Spirometry)
| Test | Criteria | Interpretation |
|------|----------|----------------|
| Post-bronchodilator FEV1/FVC | <0.70 (fixed ratio) | Confirms persistent airflow obstruction |
| Bronchodilator Reversibility | <12% or <200 mL change in FEV1 | Non-reversible (distinguishes from asthma) |

## Severity Classification (GOLD / ICMR Post-BD FEV1)
| Stage | Severity | FEV1 (% Predicted) |
|-------|----------|--------------------|
| Stage 1 | Mild | ≥80% |
| Stage 2 | Moderate | 50%–79% |
| Stage 3 | Severe | 30%–49% |
| Stage 4 | Very Severe | <30% |

## India-Specific Context & Environmental Triggers
- **Biomass Smoke Exposure**: Chronic exposure to chulha smoke (wood, cow dung, crop residue) in poorly ventilated kitchens is the primary non-smoking cause in Indian rural women.
- **Seasonal AQI Surges**: Severe winter pollution (AQI >300, PM2.5 spikes from stubble burning and crackers) causes severe exacerbations.
- **Inhaler Technique**: Your doctor may consider inhaled bronchodilators (LABA/LAMA) and inhaled corticosteroids via metered-dose inhalers with spacers or dry powder inhalers.

## ⚠️ Emergency Signs — Call 112 Immediately
- Inability to speak in full sentences due to severe breathlessness
- Cyanosis (bluish tint on lips, tongue, or nail beds)
- Sudden confusion, drowsiness, or lethargy (carbon dioxide retention)
- SpO2 drops below 88% on room air or no relief after rescue inhaler

## Lifestyle & Preventive Recommendations
- Transition to clean LPG cooking fuels (Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana).
- Remain indoors with windows closed on Poor to Severe AQI days; wear certified N95 masks if outdoors.
- Practice gentle pursed-lip breathing exercises and pulmonary rehabilitation.
