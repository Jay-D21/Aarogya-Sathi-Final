# Chronic Kidney Disease — ICMR Management Guidelines

## Definition
Abnormalities of kidney structure or function present for >3 months, with health implications. Diabetes mellitus (diabetic nephropathy) and hypertension are the leading causes of end-stage renal disease in India.

## Diagnostic Criteria & Staging (KDIGO / ICMR)
| Stage | Description | eGFR (mL/min/1.73 m²) | Urine Albumin-to-Creatinine (uACR) |
|-------|-------------|-----------------------|------------------------------------|
| Stage 1 | Kidney damage with normal eGFR | ≥90 | ≥30 mg/g |
| Stage 2 | Kidney damage with mild ↓ eGFR | 60–89 | ≥30 mg/g |
| Stage 3a/b | Moderate reduction in eGFR | 30–59 | Any |
| Stage 4 | Severe reduction in eGFR | 15–29 | Any |
| Stage 5 | Kidney Failure (End-Stage Renal Disease) | <15 | Dialysis / Transplant needed |

## India-Specific Context & Dietary Restrictions
- **Electrolyte & Protein Management**: Limit high-potassium Indian foods (tender coconut water, bananas, mangoes, spinach); leach vegetables by boiling and discarding water.
- **Sodium & Fluid Control**: Strict salt restriction (<2 g/day; avoid papad, pickles, namkeen); restrict fluids as advised by nephrologist.
- **Nephrotoxic Avoidance**: **Strictly avoid starfruit (*kamrakh*)** due to neurotoxic/nephrotoxic oxalates; avoid unprescribed painkiller NSAIDs and untested herbal compounds.
- **Renal Replacement Therapy**: Pradhan Mantri National Dialysis Programme (PMNDP) provides subsidized hemodialysis across district hospitals.

## Medical Management Considerations
- Your doctor may consider ACE inhibitors/ARBs for albuminuria reduction, SGLT2 inhibitors, and careful dose-adjustment or discontinuation of metformin when eGFR <30 mL/min.

## ⚠️ Emergency Signs — Call 112 Immediately
- Pulmonary edema: severe breathlessness, unable to lie flat, pink frothy sputum
- Hyperkalemia signs: severe muscle weakness, palpitations, irregular heartbeat
- Uremic symptoms: persistent intractable vomiting, confusion, seizure, or twitching
