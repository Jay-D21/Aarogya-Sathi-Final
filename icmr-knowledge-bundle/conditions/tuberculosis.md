# Tuberculosis — ICMR Management Guidelines

## Definition
A chronic infectious disease caused by *Mycobacterium tuberculosis*, primarily affecting the lungs (pulmonary TB) but capable of infecting any organ (extra-pulmonary TB).

## Diagnostic Criteria (NTEP Algorithm)
| Modality | Indication | Diagnostic Threshold |
|----------|------------|----------------------|
| NAAT (CBNAAT / Truenat) | Preferred upfront test | Detects *M. tb* DNA and Rifampicin resistance |
| Sputum Smear Microscopy | Smear microscopy (ZN stain) | Acid-fast bacilli (AFB) detected |
| Chest Radiograph (CXR) | Initial triage / screening | Cavitary lesions, infiltrates, or effusion |
| Mantoux Tuberculin Skin Test | Latent infection / pediatric TB | Induration ≥10 mm considered positive |

## Classification & Regimens
- **Drug-Sensitive TB (DS-TB)**: Susceptible to all first-line drugs; 6-month standardized fixed-dose combination (HRZE).
- **Multi-Drug Resistant TB (MDR-TB)**: Resistant to at least Isoniazid and Rifampicin; managed with second-line oral regimens (Bedaquiline-based).
- **Extra-Pulmonary TB (EPTB)**: Pleural, lymph node, central nervous system, or skeletal involvement.

## India-Specific Context & Public Health Protocol
- **NTEP & Nikshay Portal**: Mandatory notification of all diagnosed cases; eligible patients receive nutritional direct benefit transfer (Nikshay Poshan Yojana).
- **Contact Tracing**: Household contacts, especially children <5 years and immunocompromised individuals, must undergo active screening and TB preventive therapy (TPT).
- **Nutritional Support**: High-protein Indian foods—sattu, boiled pulses, eggs, curd, paneer, and milk—aid tissue repair and immunity.
- **Medical Regimen**: Never stop anti-TB medications prematurely to prevent drug resistance. Your doctor may consider individualized drug monitoring.

## ⚠️ Emergency Signs — Call 112 Immediately
- Massive hemoptysis (coughing up >100 mL of frank blood)
- Severe sudden-onset chest pain and breathlessness (tension pneumothorax)
- Stupor, altered sensorium, or projectile vomiting (tubercular meningitis)
