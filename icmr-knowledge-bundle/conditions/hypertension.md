# Hypertension

## Definition
Persistently elevated arterial blood pressure.

## Classification
| Category | Systolic (mmHg) | Diastolic (mmHg) |
|----------|----------------|------------------|
| Normal | <120 | <80 |
| Elevated | 120-129 | <80 |
| Stage 1 HTN | 130-139 | 80-89 |
| Stage 2 HTN | ≥140 | ≥90 |
| Crisis | >180 | >120 |

## First-Line Treatment
- Lifestyle modification (DASH diet, reduce sodium, exercise)
- ACE inhibitors or ARBs for most patients
- Calcium channel blockers as alternative

See also: [Atenolol](../drugs/atenolol.md), [Amlodipine](../drugs/amlodipine.md)

## Monitoring
- BP check at every visit
- Home BP monitoring recommended
- Annual kidney function, electrolytes
