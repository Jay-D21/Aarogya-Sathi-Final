# Obesity — ICMR Management Guidelines

## Definition
A chronic condition of abnormal or excessive adiposity impairing health. South Asians have higher body fat percentage and visceral fat at lower BMI values ("thin-fat Indian" phenotype).

## Diagnostic Criteria (Asian Cutoffs)
| Parameter | Normal | Overweight | Obese |
|-----------|--------|------------|-------|
| Asian BMI (kg/m²) | 18.5–22.9 | 23.0–27.4 | ≥27.5 |
| Waist (Men) | <90 cm | 90–99 cm | ≥100 cm |
| Waist (Women) | <80 cm | 80–89 cm | ≥90 cm |

## Severity Classification
- **Class I Obesity**: BMI 27.5–32.4 kg/m²
- **Class II Obesity**: BMI 32.5–37.4 kg/m²
- **Class III (Severe/Morbid)**: BMI ≥37.5 kg/m² or BMI ≥32.5 kg/m² with major comorbidities

## India-Specific Context & Diet Modifications
- **Millets Integration**: Replace refined wheat/maida and polished white rice with whole millets (jowar, bajra, ragi, foxtail millet).
- **Plate Method**: Half plate green sabzi/salad, one quarter dal/sprouts/curd, one quarter millet roti or brown rice.
- **Fat Quality**: Limit vanaspati, ghee, and deep-fried snacks (samosa, pakora); use cold-pressed mustard, peanut, or sunflower oil in moderation (<15–20 mL/day).
- **Cultural Practices**: Walk 15–20 minutes post-meals (Shatapawali); ensure 150+ minutes of moderate activity per week.

## Medical Management Considerations
- Your doctor may consider anti-obesity pharmacotherapy or bariatric evaluation if lifestyle modifications do not achieve target weight reduction.

## ⚠️ Red Flags & Emergency Signs — Call 112
- Sudden crushing chest pain radiating to neck or left arm
- Acute breathlessness or severe orthopnea at rest
- Sudden limb weakness, slurred speech, or facial droop
