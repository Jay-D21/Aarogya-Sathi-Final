# Thyroid Disorders — ICMR Management Guidelines

## Definition
Endocrine disorders caused by dysregulated production of thyroid hormones: hypothyroidism (underactive thyroid, most prevalent in India) and hyperthyroidism (overactive thyroid).

## Diagnostic Criteria
| Condition | Serum TSH | Free T4 | Free T3 |
|-----------|-----------|---------|---------|
| Overt Hypothyroidism | >4.5 mIU/L (Elevated) | Decreased | Normal or Low |
| Subclinical Hypothyroidism | >4.5 mIU/L (Elevated) | Normal | Normal |
| Overt Hyperthyroidism | <0.4 mIU/L (Suppressed) | Elevated | Elevated |
| Subclinical Hyperthyroidism | <0.4 mIU/L (Suppressed) | Normal | Normal |

## Clinical Classification & Presentation
- **Hypothyroidism**: Fatigue, weight gain, cold intolerance, dry skin, constipation, puffy face, heavy periods.
- **Hyperthyroidism**: Palpitations, unexplained weight loss, tremors, heat intolerance, anxiety, frequent bowel movements.

## India-Specific Context & Nutrition
- **Iodine Status**: Universal salt iodization under the National Iodine Deficiency Disorders Control Programme (NIDDCP) has drastically reduced endemic goiters, but sub-Himalayan and riverine belts remain historically vulnerable.
- **Dietary Considerations**: Consume adequate iodized salt; cook goitrogenic foods (cabbage/patta gobhi, cauliflower/phool gobhi, mustard greens) thoroughly rather than raw.
- **Medical Therapy**: Your doctor may consider daily levothyroxine on an empty stomach (30–60 minutes before morning tea/breakfast) for hypothyroidism, or antithyroid medications for hyperthyroidism.

## Monitoring Protocol
- Retest serum TSH 6–8 weeks after dosage adjustment, then every 6–12 months once stable.
- Regular antenatal thyroid screening: target TSH <2.5 mIU/L in the first trimester.

## ⚠️ Emergency Signs — Call 112 Immediately
- **Myxedema Coma**: Severe hypothermia, profound lethargy, bradycardia, confusion.
- **Thyroid Storm**: High fever, extreme agitation, delirium, tachycardia (>140 bpm), arrhythmia.
