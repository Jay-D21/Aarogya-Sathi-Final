# Acute Chest Pain and STEMI Protocol

## Overview & STEMI Signs
Acute chest discomfort often indicates Acute Coronary Syndrome (ACS) or ST-Elevation Myocardial Infarction (STEMI).
Key signs include central retrosternal crushing or squeezing chest pain radiating to left arm, neck, jaw, or epigastrium, accompanied by profuse diaphoresis, dyspnea, and nausea.

## Diagnostic Criteria & Clinical Differentiation
| Parameter | Angina Pectoris | STEMI / Acute Myocardial Infarction |
|---|---|---|
| Pain Quality | Squeezing, heaviness, tightness | Severe crushing, pressure, band-like constriction |
| Radiation | Left shoulder, neck, lower jaw | Left or both arms, jaw, back, epigastrium |
| Duration | <15 minutes; eases with rest | >20-30 minutes; unremitting and constant |
| Associated Signs | Mild shortness of breath | Cold diaphoresis, nausea, syncope, presyncope |

## Severity Classification
- **Low Risk**: Sharp, pleuritic, positional chest wall tenderness; fleeting stabbing pain.
- **Intermediate Risk**: Known CAD with typical angina pattern responding promptly to rest.
- **High Risk / STEMI**: Continuous pain >20 minutes, diaphoresis, hypotension, or cardiac arrhythmia.

## India-Specific Context
- South Asians present with CAD 10-15 years earlier than Western cohorts due to metabolic risk profiles.
- Asian BMI cutoff criteria apply: Overweight ≥23 kg/m², Obese ≥25 kg/m²; waist circumference >90 cm (men), >80 cm (women).
- Misconception: Chest discomfort is frequently dismissed as "gas" or "acidity" after heavy meals, critically delaying medical care.
- Dietary factors: Replace trans-fat cooking oils with high-fiber roti, moong dal, seasonal sabzi, and millets.

## Immediate Action & Call 112 Protocol
1. **Call 112 Immediately**: Request an advanced life support cardiac ambulance.
2. **DO NOT DRIVE YOURSELF**: Driving or exertion increases myocardial oxygen demand; wait for emergency EMS.
3. **Rest & Recline**: Keep patient in a semi-upright (Fowler's) position; loosen collars and tight clothing.
4. **Aspirin Protocol**: Only if explicitly instructed by a physician or emergency tele-triage, your doctor may consider chewing a dispersible aspirin tablet (do not swallow whole; avoid if history of active ulcer bleeding or allergy).

## ⚠️ Emergency Signs — Call 112
- Crushing retrosternal chest pain lasting longer than 15 minutes
- Cold clammy sweats with sudden dizziness or fainting
- Severe breathlessness with inability to speak in sentences
- Sudden drop in blood pressure or pulse irregularity
