# Heatstroke Emergency Protocol

## Overview & Recognition
Heatstroke is an acute medical emergency occurring when thermoregulation fails and core body temperature exceeds 40°C (104°F), accompanied by central nervous system (CNS) dysfunction and multi-organ failure.

## Diagnostic Criteria & Differential Triage
| Parameter | Heat Exhaustion | Heatstroke (Life-Threatening) |
|---|---|---|
| Core Body Temp | 38°C - 40°C (100.4°F - 104°F) | >40°C (>104°F) rectally / core |
| CNS Dysfunction | Intact or mild dizziness/fatigue | Confusion, delirium, seizures, stupor, coma |
| Skin / Sweating | Profuse sweating, pale, cool | Dry/hot skin (classic) or sweating (exertional) |
| Systemic Impact | Mild dehydration, hemoconcentration | Rhabdomyolysis, renal failure, DIC, shock |

## Severity Classification
- **Mild Heat Illness**: Heat rash (prickly heat/miliaria), heat cramps in calf/abdominal muscles, heat edema.
- **Heat Exhaustion**: Tachycardia, dizziness, headache, intense thirst, nausea; mentation remains intact.
- **Heatstroke**: Core temp >40°C with altered sensorium, ataxia, seizures, or cardiovascular collapse.

## India-Specific Context & Vulnerable Groups
- Extreme climate: Summer temperatures consistently reach 42°C to 47°C+ across northern, central, and western India.
- High-risk populations: Construction workers, farm laborers, brick kiln workers, traffic police, and gig delivery riders.
- Rehydration & traditional foods: Provide Oral Rehydration Salts (ORS), cold thin chaas (salted buttermilk), coconut water, and aam panna (boiled raw mango cooler); avoid sugary sodas, strong chai, and alcohol.
- Dietary recovery: Light meals with moong dal khichdi, curd, and bottle gourd (lauki) sabzi; avoid heavy fried pakoras.

## Immediate Action & Call 112 Protocol
1. **Call 112 Immediately**: Heatstroke is rapidly fatal without advanced ICU management; request an ambulance.
2. **Move to Shade / Cool Space**: Shift patient immediately out of the sun into an air-conditioned room or cool shade.
3. **Aggressive Active Cooling**: Strip heavy clothing; douse or mist whole body with cool/tap water and fan continuously; place cloth-wrapped ice packs on neck, armpits, and groin.
4. **Hydration Caution**: Administer chilled ORS or salted lemon water in small sips ONLY if patient is fully conscious and alert.
5. **DO NOT Give Oral Fluids If Altered**: If patient is confused, delirious, or unconscious, NEVER force fluids (aspiration risk).

## ⚠️ Emergency Signs — Call 112
- Core temperature exceeding 40°C (104°F) with hot, flushed skin
- Unconsciousness, coma, hallucinations, or generalized seizures
- Rapid, bounding pulse degenerating into a weak, thready pulse
- Complete cessation of urination or vomiting preventing fluid retention
