# Hypoglycemia Emergency

## Recognition
- Blood glucose <70 mg/dL
- Symptoms: trembling, sweating, confusion, dizziness, hunger
- Severe: seizures, loss of consciousness

## Immediate Action
1. If conscious: Give 15-20g fast-acting glucose (3-4 glucose tablets, half cup juice, 1 tablespoon sugar in water)
2. Wait 15 minutes, recheck glucose
3. If still <70 mg/dL: repeat step 1
4. Once normalized: eat a snack with protein (biscuits with peanut butter)

## When to Call Emergency (112)
- Patient is unconscious
- Patient cannot swallow
- Glucose remains <50 mg/dL after 2 treatments
- Seizure activity

## Prevention
- Regular meal timing — do not skip meals
- Monitor glucose before and after exercise
- Carry glucose tablets or sugar sachets at all times
- Educate family members on recognizing symptoms
