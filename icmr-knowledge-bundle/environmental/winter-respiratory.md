# Winter Smog and Respiratory Health

## Overview & The North Indian Smog Crisis
During winter months (November to February), thermal inversion traps atmospheric particulates across the Indo-Gangetic Plain (Delhi NCR, Punjab, Haryana, UP, Bihar). Stubble burning, traffic emissions, construction dust, and cold fog combine into dense toxic smog, driving the National Air Quality Index (NAQI) into "Severe" categories (>400).

## Indian NAQI Scale & Clinical Respiratory Risk
| NAQI Category | AQI Range | 24-hr PM2.5 (µg/m³) | Clinical Impact & Vulnerability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Good / Satisfactory | 0 - 100 | 0 - 60 | Minimal impact; minor breathing discomfort in sensitive people |
| Moderate | 101 - 200 | 61 - 90 | Wheezing and dyspnea in patients with asthma and COPD |
| Poor | 201 - 300 | 91 - 120 | Widespread breathing discomfort upon prolonged outdoor exposure |
| Very Poor | 301 - 400 | 121 - 250 | Triggers acute exacerbations in chronic respiratory patients |
| Severe / Hazardous | 401 - 500+ | >250 | Severe respiratory distress in patients; healthy individuals impacted |

## Severity Classification of Respiratory Exacerbation
- **Mild Exacerbation**: Occasional cough, mild throat irritation, peak expiratory flow (PEF) >80%.
- **Moderate Exacerbation**: Persistent wheeze, cough disturbing sleep, PEF 60-80%, rescue inhaler needed >3 times/day.
- **Severe Exacerbation**: Stridor, speaking only in single words, silent chest, tachypnea (>30/min), SpO2 <90%.

## Mask Guidance & Indoor Air Quality
- **Mask Guidance**: Cloth masks and surgical masks DO NOT filter ultrafine PM2.5 particles. Wear a certified, well-fitted N95 or FFP2 respirator mask when stepping outdoors.
- **Reschedule Outdoor Exercise**: Cancel morning walks and jogs between 5:00 AM and 9:30 AM when toxic particulates settle near ground level. Exercise indoors (yoga, pranayama).
- **Indoor Pollution Control**: Ban indoor burning of dhoop, agarbatti (incense sticks), mosquito coils, and unvented wood/coal chulhas. Keep windows sealed during peak smog hours; run HEPA air purifiers.
- **Dietary & Lifestyle Measures**: Drink warm fluids and herbal tulsi-ginger decoctions. Eat warm, nutrient-dense meals featuring bajra or jowar roti, moong dal, steamed winter sabzi, and fresh curd at room temperature.

## Medical Management & When to Call 112
- Respiratory patients must carry prescribed rescue inhalers; your doctor may consider stepped-up controller inhaler regimens during winter.
- **⚠️ Call 112 Immediately**: Inability to speak full sentences, blue discoloration around lips/nails (cyanosis), severe intercostal retractions, or SpO2 dropping below 90%.
