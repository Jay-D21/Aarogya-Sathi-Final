# Heat Health and Heatwave Management

## Overview & Indian Heatwave Criteria
The India Meteorological Department (IMD) and NDMA define heatwaves based on departure from normal maximum temperatures:
- **Plains**: Max temperature ≥40°C (or departure ≥4.5°C above normal).
- **Hills & Coastal Areas**: Max temperature ≥30°C in hills, ≥37°C in coastal stations.
- **Severe Heatwave**: Departure ≥6.4°C or absolute maximum temperature ≥47°C.

## IMD Heat Alert Protocol & Public Actions
| Alert Level | Temperature Condition | Recommended Public Health Action |
|---|---|---|
| Green (Normal) | Normal summer range | Maintain baseline hydration; normal outdoor routine |
| Yellow (Watch) | Heatwave lasting 1-2 days | High-risk groups limit midday sun exposure (12 PM - 3 PM) |
| Orange (Alert) | Persistent heatwave 3-4 days | Reschedule outdoor work; ensure cool rest areas and ORS |
| Red (Warning) | Extreme heatwave >4 days | Suspend outdoor heavy physical labor; emergency cooling centers |

## Severity Classification of Heat Illness
- **Mild Heat Strain**: Heat rash, mild swelling of feet (heat edema), transient muscle cramps.
- **Moderate Strain (Heat Exhaustion)**: Dizziness, profuse sweating, nausea, tachycardia, preserved cognition.
- **Severe Crisis (Heatstroke)**: Core body temperature >40°C, altered mental state, delirium, or syncope.

## Vulnerable Groups & Work Modifications
- **Vulnerable Groups**: Agricultural laborers, MGNREGA workers, gig workers, traffic police, elderly (>65 years), infants, and patients with CKD, hypertension, or heart disease.
- **Work Modifications**: Reschedule heavy manual work to cooler hours (6:00 AM - 10:30 AM and after 5:00 PM). Mandatory shaded rest breaks every 45-60 minutes.
- **Hydration & Indian Dietary Support**: Drink 3-4 liters of water daily. Consume natural hydrators like thin salted chaas (buttermilk), roasted jeera lemon water, sattu sharbat, and boiled aam panna.
- **Meals**: Eat light, cooling home-cooked meals including moong dal, bottle gourd (lauki) sabzi, cucumber, curd, and millets (jowar). Avoid greasy street foods, excess tea, and sugary carbonated drinks.

## ⚠️ Emergency Signs — Call 112 Protocol
- Core temperature >40°C (>104°F) with hot, dry, or profusely sweaty skin
- Confusion, disorientation, slurred speech, or loss of consciousness
- Inability to retain fluids with continuous vomiting
- *Action*: Immediately move victim to shade, apply ice packs to axillae/groin, douse with cold water, and **Call 112**.
