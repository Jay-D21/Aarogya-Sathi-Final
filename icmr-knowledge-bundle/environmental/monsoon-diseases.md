# Monsoon Diseases Prevention and Management

## Overview & Seasonal Epidemiology
The Indian southwest monsoon creates extensive waterlogging and humidity, triggering simultaneous epidemics of vector-borne infections (Dengue, Malaria, Chikungunya) and water-borne enteric diseases (Leptospirosis, Typhoid, Acute Gastroenteritis, Hepatitis A/E).

## Diagnostic Criteria & Clinical Profiles
| Condition | Pathogen / Route | Distinctive Symptoms | High-Risk Alert Indicators |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dengue | Dengue virus (*Aedes* mosquito) | Retro-orbital pain, high fever, severe myalgia ("breakbone") | Rapid platelet drop, hemoconcentration, mucosal bleeding |
| Malaria | *Plasmodium* (*Anopheles* mosquito) | Paroxysms of shaking chills, high fever, drenching sweats | Blackwater fever, altered sensorium (*P. falciparum*) |
| Leptospirosis | *Leptospira* via floodwater | Severe calf muscle tenderness, conjunctival suffusion | Jaundice, oliguria, pulmonary hemorrhage (Weil's disease) |
| Typhoid / Cholera | Contaminated water/food | Step-ladder fever, "rice-water" diarrhea, abdominal colic | Severe dehydration, shock, intestinal perforation |

## Severity Classification
- **Mild / Outpatient**: Low-grade fever, tolerable joint ache, absence of warning signs, stable vitals.
- **Moderate / Observation**: Persistent vomiting, abdominal tenderness, platelet count 50,000-100,000/µL, mild dehydration.
- **Severe / Critical**: Dengue Shock Syndrome (DSS), cerebral malaria, anuria (<0.5 mL/kg/h), or uncompensated metabolic acidosis.

## India-Specific Context & Prevention
- **Vector Control**: Eliminate stagnant water weekly in desert coolers, construction sumps, discarded coconut shells, and overhead tanks. Use mosquito nets and apply DEET/picaridin repellents.
- **Floodwater Caution**: Avoid wading barefoot in flooded roads or agricultural runoffs; workers in waterlogged areas should wear high rubber gumboots to prevent Leptospirosis.
- **Water & Food Sanitation**: Drink only boiled water or reliable RO filtered water. Strictly avoid cut roadside fruits, sugarcane juice, and street chaat.
- **Dietary Support**: Consume freshly prepared, easily digestible meals: moong dal khichdi, thin vegetable soups, curd, soft roti, and steamed lauki or ridge gourd sabzi. Maintain hydration with ORS and tender coconut water.

## ⚠️ Emergency Signs — Call 112 Protocol
- Active bleeding: Epistaxis (nosebleeds), bleeding gums, black tarry stools (melena), or blood in vomitus
- Sudden collapse, cold clammy extremities, or systolic BP <90 mmHg
- Severe breathlessness, persistent uncontrolled vomiting, or seizures
- Complete lack of urination (>8-12 hours) or yellowish discoloration of eyes (jaundice) with disorientation
