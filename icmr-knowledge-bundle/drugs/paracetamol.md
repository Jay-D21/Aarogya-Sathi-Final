# Paracetamol (Acetaminophen)

## Class & Mechanism
Analgesic and antipyretic. Centrally inhibits prostaglandin synthesis and modulates the hypothalamic thermoregulatory center to reduce fever and alleviate mild-to-moderate pain.

## Clinical Indications & Physician Role
- First-line choice for symptomatic relief of fever, headache, body ache, and osteoarthritis.
- Your doctor may consider paracetamol spaced at intervals of at least 4 to 6 hours, adhering strictly to safe daily limits based on body weight and liver function.

## Critical Warnings: Liver Toxicity & Alcohol Interaction
- **Hepatotoxicity Warning**: Overdose depletes hepatic glutathione, causing toxic NAPQI metabolite accumulation and fatal acute hepatic necrosis.
- **Strictly Avoid with Alcohol**: Combining alcohol with paracetamol significantly elevates liver injury risk and reduces toxic clearance thresholds.
- **Hidden OTC Combinations**: Avoid concurrent cold syrups, sinus sachets, or headache balms containing undisclosed paracetamol.

## Toxicity Severity Classification
| Phase | Time Elapsed | Clinical Signs | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stage 1 | 0-24 hours | Nausea, vomiting, pallor, malaise, sweating | Emergency hospital referral; evaluate N-acetylcysteine |
| Stage 2 | 24-72 hours | Right upper quadrant abdominal pain, rising AST/ALT | Inpatient monitoring and liver protection |
| Stage 3 | >72 hours | Jaundice, coagulopathy, encephalopathy, liver failure | Critical care / Call 112 resuscitation |

## India-Specific Clinical Context
- **Preferred in Dengue & Monsoon Fevers**: Paracetamol is the safest antipyretic during India's monsoon viral outbreaks (dengue, chikungunya). NSAIDs (ibuprofen, mefenamic acid) are strictly avoided due to severe hemorrhage risks.
- **Hydration Support**: Accompany fever management with plenty of fluids—tender coconut water, nimbu pani, or light dal soup.

## Emergency Signs (Call 112)
- Intentional or accidental overdose, severe vomiting, yellow eyes (jaundice), or sudden confusion. Call 112 immediately.
