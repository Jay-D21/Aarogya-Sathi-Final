# Atenolol

## Class & Mechanism
Cardioselective beta-1 adrenergic receptor antagonist. Reduces myocardial contractility, lowers heart rate, and decreases cardiac output, resulting in blood pressure reduction and myocardial oxygen demand control.

## Clinical Role & Physician Considerations
- Management of [Hypertension](../conditions/hypertension.md), chronic stable angina, and rate control in supraventricular arrhythmias.
- Your doctor may consider atenolol when elevated resting heart rate accompanies hypertension or in post-infarction care, adjusting therapy to maintain target resting pulse.

## Critical Warning: Avoid Sudden Discontinuation
- **Never stop taking atenolol abruptly**: Abrupt cessation triggers severe rebound hypertension, rapid tachycardia, arrhythmias, or acute myocardial ischemia.
- Any tapering schedule must be strictly designed and monitored by your treating physician over 1-2 weeks.

## Adverse Effect Severity Classification
| Severity | Manifestations | Clinical Action |
|---|---|---|
| Mild / Common | Cold hands/feet, fatigue, lethargy, mild resting bradycardia | Monitor at home; report at next routine clinic visit |
| Moderate | Significant bradycardia (pulse 45-50 bpm), sleep disruption, blunted exercise tolerance | Doctor may evaluate dose reduction or switch medication |
| Severe / Emergency | Symptomatic heart block, bronchospasm, acute heart failure exacerbation | Immediate emergency medical care / hospitalization |

## India-Specific Clinical Context
- **Masking Hypoglycemia in Diabetics**: High prevalence of diabetes-hypertension comorbidity in India. Atenolol masks sympathetic warning signs of hypoglycemia (tremors, tachycardia), though sweating is preserved. Pair with frequent monitoring.
- **Diet & Lifestyle Synergy**: Support therapy with heart-healthy Indian staples like oats, millets (jowar, bajra), dal, and steamed sabzi; avoid heavily fried street snacks and unprescribed stimulant kadhas.

## Emergency Signs (Call 112)
- Heart rate dropping below 40 bpm accompanied by severe lightheadedness.
- Crushing chest pain, sudden breathlessness, or collapse. Call 112 immediately.
