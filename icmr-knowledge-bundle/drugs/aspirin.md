# Aspirin (Low-Dose)

## Class & Mechanism
Antiplatelet agent (acetylsalicylic acid). Irreversibly acetylates platelet cyclooxygenase-1 (COX-1), blocking thromboxane A2 synthesis and preventing platelet aggregation and arterial thrombosis.

## Indications & Physician Considerations
- Secondary prevention of myocardial infarction, ischemic stroke, and in patients with coronary stents or bypass grafts.
- Your doctor may consider low-dose aspirin therapy after weighing individual ischemic benefit against gastrointestinal bleeding risk.

## Critical Warning: NOT for Self-Medication
- **Do not self-prescribe aspirin**: Routine self-medication as a "heart tonic" or preventive pill without a physician's directive is dangerous.
- In primary prevention (without established cardiovascular disease), bleeding risks often outweigh benefits under ICMR guidelines.

## Bleeding Risk Severity Classification
| Severity | Manifestations | Action Required |
|---|---|---|
| Minor Bleeding | Easy bruising, minor nosebleeds (epistaxis), bleeding gums | Discuss with doctor at next scheduled visit |
| Moderate Bleeding | Persistent dyspepsia, significant hematuria, prolonged cuts bleeding | Contact treating doctor; evaluate gastric protection |
| Major / Emergency | Black tarry stools (melena), vomiting blood, acute hemorrhagic stroke | Stop drug immediately; emergency hospital admission |

## India-Specific Clinical Context
- **Gastric Irritation & Diet**: Spiced gravies, pickles, and excessive black tea can exacerbate aspirin-induced gastritis. Take aspirin with or directly after a balanced meal (dal, khichdi, curd, or roti).
- **Dental & Ayurvedic Interactions**: Inform your dentist prior to extractions. Disclose concurrent use of herbal preparations (e.g., ginkgo, garlic extracts, ginger teas) that can intensify bleeding tendencies.

## Emergency Signs (Call 112)
- Vomiting coffee-ground material or bright red blood.
- Sudden numbness or paralysis of the face, arm, or leg. Call 112 immediately.
