# Amlodipine

## Class & Mechanism
Long-acting dihydropyridine calcium channel blocker (CCB). Inhibits calcium influx into vascular smooth muscle, promoting peripheral arterial vasodilation and reducing systemic blood pressure.

## Indications & Physician Role
- First-line monotherapy or combination therapy for essential [Hypertension](../conditions/hypertension.md) and chronic stable angina.
- Your doctor may consider amlodipine as a once-daily regimen, adjusting treatment based on BP targets and cardiovascular risk profile.

## Adverse Effect Severity Classification
| Severity | Manifestations | Action Required |
|---|---|---|
| Mild / Common | Peripheral pedal edema (ankle swelling), flushing, headache | Report at routine follow-up; evaluate salt intake |
| Moderate | Persistent dizziness, postural hypotension, palpitations | Doctor may consider dose modification or combination agent |
| Severe / Rare | Severe hypotension, syncope, generalized allergic reaction | Discontinue under guidance; urgent medical evaluation |

## India-Specific Clinical Context
- **Pedal Edema Misconception**: Ankle swelling is common in warm Indian climates and prolonged standing; patients frequently mistake this for kidney failure. Clinical reassurance and review are vital.
- **High Sodium Intake**: Traditional Indian diets rich in pickles (achaar), papad, and namkeen blunt CCB efficacy. Advise balanced meals (dal, roti, sabzi, curd) prepared with minimal added salt.
- **Climate Interaction**: High ambient summer temperatures (>40°C) cause physiological vasodilation, increasing risk of orthostatic dizziness on standing.

## What to Tell Your Doctor
- Development of noticeable ankle or foot swelling.
- Dizziness when standing up from sitting or lying down.
- Concurrent use of ayurvedic formulations, herbal supplements, or other anti-hypertensives.

## Emergency Signs (Call 112)
- Sudden chest pain radiating to left arm or neck.
- Severe shortness of breath, fainting (syncope), or systolic BP >180 mmHg (Hypertensive Crisis). Call 112 immediately.
