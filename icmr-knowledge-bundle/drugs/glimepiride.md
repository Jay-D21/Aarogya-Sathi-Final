# Glimepiride

## Class & Mechanism
Second/third-generation sulfonylurea oral antidiabetic. Stimulates pancreatic beta-cell insulin secretion by binding to sulfonylurea receptors and closing ATP-sensitive potassium channels.

## Clinical Role & Physician Guidance
- Indicated in [Type 2 Diabetes](../conditions/diabetes-type2.md) when glycemic control is suboptimal with lifestyle measures and first-line therapy.
- Your doctor may consider glimepiride either as monotherapy or in fixed-dose combination with metformin, titrating cautiously against blood glucose readings.

## Administration: Take with Breakfast
- Always take immediately before or with the first substantial meal of the day (e.g., breakfast of poha, idli-sambar, or whole wheat roti).
- **Never take glimepiride on an empty stomach** or if you plan to skip a meal, as prolonged hypoglycemia can develop rapidly.

## Hypoglycemia Severity Classification
| Severity | Typical Manifestations | Immediate Action |
|---|---|---|
| Mild | Tremors, sweating, palpitations, intense hunger | 15g fast sugar (3 glucose tabs / sugar water); recheck in 15 min |
| Moderate | Dizziness, headache, blurred vision, confusion | Consume fast sugar followed by complex carbohydrates (roti, dal) |
| Severe | Stupor, seizures, loss of consciousness | Emergency medical event; call 112 immediately |

## India-Specific Context & Fasting
- **Religious & Cultural Fasts**: Fasting (e.g., Karwa Chauth, Navratri, Ramadan, Ekadashi) is widespread in India. Sulfonylureas taken during prolonged fasting cause dangerous drops in blood glucose. Consult your doctor beforehand to adjust or omit doses.
- **Elderly & Renal Impairment**: Clearance is reduced in older Indian adults; careful monitoring for nocturnal hypoglycemia is recommended.

## Emergency Signs (Call 112)
- Profound lethargy, unresponsiveness, or recurrent seizures secondary to hypoglycemia. Call 112 immediately.
