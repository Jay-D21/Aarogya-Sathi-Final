# Sleep Hygiene & Rest Guidelines

## Overview & Optimal Duration
Adequate, restful sleep of 7 to 8 hours per night is a foundational pillar of metabolic and cardiovascular health under ICMR 2023 guidelines. Chronic sleep deprivation (<6 hours) induces sustained sympathetic overdrive and systemic inflammation.

## Impact on Chronic Diseases
| Health Condition | Physiological Impact of Inadequate Sleep |
|---|---|
| [Type 2 Diabetes](../conditions/diabetes-type2.md) | Elevates morning cortisol, impairs insulin sensitivity, and raises fasting blood glucose |
| [Hypertension](../conditions/hypertension.md) | Blunts normal nocturnal blood pressure "dipping," keeping nighttime BP elevated |
| Obesity (Asian BMI ≥25 kg/m²) | Disrupts appetite hormones (leptin/ghrelin), triggering late-night carbohydrate cravings |

## Indian Cultural & Environmental Context
- **Joint Family Dynamics**: Communal living often delays evening meals past 9:30 PM. Strive to finish a light dinner (dal, roti, steamed sabzi, curd) at least 2 hours before sleeping.
- **Ambient Urban Noise**: Street traffic, loudspeakers, and nearby activity disrupt sleep architecture. Use earplugs or constant fan hum (white noise) to mask ambient sounds.
- **Excessive Screen Time**: Widespread late-night mobile phone browsing in bed emits blue light, suppressing natural melatonin synthesis and delaying sleep onset.

## Actionable Sleep Strategies
1. **Screen Curfew**: Discontinue smartphones, tablets, and television at least 45–60 minutes before bedtime.
2. **Consistent Sleep Schedule**: Maintain identical sleep and wake times daily, even on weekends.
3. **Stimulant Restriction**: Avoid evening chai, coffee, and tobacco after 6:00 PM.
4. **Relaxing Pre-Bed Routine**: Engage in light stretching, warm foot wash, or gentle reading rather than stimulating media.

## Warning Signs & When to Seek Medical Care
- Chronic loud snoring with daytime exhaustion or witnessed pauses in breathing suggests Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA). Your doctor may consider a formal polysomnography study.
- **Emergency Signs (Call 112)**: Sudden nocturnal awakening with severe crushing chest pain, radiating arm pain, or acute shortness of breath. Call 112 immediately.
