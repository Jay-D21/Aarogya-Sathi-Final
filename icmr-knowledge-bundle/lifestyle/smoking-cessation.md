# Tobacco & Smoking Cessation

## Burden & ICMR Statistics
Tobacco consumption is the single most preventable cause of death in India, responsible for over 1.35 million deaths annually (ICMR-NCDIR 2023). Tobacco use drastically accelerates endothelial damage, precipitating premature heart attacks, strokes, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, and oral and lung cancers.

## Forms of Tobacco Prevalent in India
| Tobacco Form | Composition & Usage | Specific Health Hazards |
|---|---|---|
| **Bidi** | Sun-cured tobacco flake rolled in tendu leaf | Delivers 3x more nicotine and 5x more carbon monoxide than manufactured cigarettes |
| **Cigarettes** | Cured tobacco paper-wrapped | Accelerated coronary atherosclerosis, arterial stiffening, and [Hypertension](../conditions/hypertension.md) |
| **Smokeless (Gutka, Khaini, Zarda)** | Tobacco with slaked lime, areca nut, flavourings | High risk of oral submucous fibrosis (OSMF) and aggressive oral squamous cell carcinoma |

## Clinical Cessation Strategies
- **National Tobacco Quitline**: Call toll-free **1800-112-356** for confidential counseling in multiple Indian languages, or register via SMS 'QUIT' to 56349.
- **Pharmacotherapy & NRT**: Your doctor may consider Nicotine Replacement Therapy (nicotine gums or transdermal patches) or non-nicotine oral medications to ease withdrawal cravings.
- **Behavioral Habit Reversal**: Tobacco cravings often follow meals (dal-roti, rice) or tea breaks. Substitute with cardamom (elaichi), roasted fennel (saunf), or cinnamon sticks.

## Red Flag Symptoms
- Chronic white/red patches in the oral cavity (leukoplakia/erythroplakia) or reduced mouth opening.
- Persistent hoarseness or coughing for >2 weeks requires urgent specialist evaluation.

## Emergency Signs (Call 112)
- Sudden squeezing chest pressure, pain radiating to left shoulder or jaw, or acute breathlessness. Call 112 immediately.
