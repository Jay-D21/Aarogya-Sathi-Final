# Exercise Guidelines — ICMR Recommendations

## General Indian Adult Recommendations
- **Minimum**: 150 minutes moderate-intensity activity per week (ICMR 2023)
- **Optimal**: 300 minutes/week for additional health benefits
- **Strength training**: 2 sessions/week for all adults ≥18

## Exercise by Health Condition

### Diabetes
- 30 minutes brisk walking DAILY — most effective single intervention
- Blood sugar: Check before exercise; postpone if <80 mg/dL or >250 mg/dL
- Best time: 1-2 hours after a meal (stabilizes post-meal sugar spike)
- Carry glucose biscuits/sweets in case of hypoglycemia during exercise

### Hypertension
- Aerobic exercise (walking, swimming, cycling): 30-45 min/day, 5 days/week
- **Avoid**: Heavy weight lifting, straining (raises BP acutely)
- Target HR during exercise: 50-70% of maximum (max HR = 220 - age)
- Start slow if newly diagnosed; build intensity over 4-6 weeks

### Heart Disease (post-event)
- Only after cardiologist clearance
- Begin with 10-min walks, gradually increase
- Stop if: chest pain, breathlessness, palpitations, dizziness

### Obesity (BMI > 27.5 for Indians)
- Start with low-impact: walking, swimming, cycling
- 60 min/day recommended for weight loss
- Indian millets (jowar, bajra, ragi) + exercise combination most effective

## AQI-Based Exercise Guidelines
| AQI Level | Exercise Recommendation |
|-----------|------------------------|
| Good (0-50) | All outdoor activities safe |
| Satisfactory (51-100) | Normal outdoor activities OK |
| Moderate (101-200) | Reduce duration/intensity; sensitive groups exercise indoors |
| Poor (201-300) | Move exercise indoors; avoid vigorous outdoor activity |
| Severe (301-400) | No outdoor exercise; indoor only |
| Hazardous (>400) | No exercise even indoors (open windows) |

## Yoga and Traditional Indian Exercise
- Surya Namaskar: Full-body cardiovascular and strength — suitable for diabetes, obesity
- Pranayama (breathing exercises): Particularly beneficial for asthma patients
- Walking (early morning, parks): Most accessible — ICMR primary recommendation

## Warning Signs to Stop Exercise Immediately
- Chest pain or pressure
- Dizziness or lightheadedness
- Severe shortness of breath
- Heart palpitations
- Unusual sweating

→ If any of above occur: Sit down, rest, and call your doctor.
