# Diet for Diabetes

## Key Principles
- Reduce refined carbohydrates and sugars
- Include high-fiber foods (whole grains, legumes, vegetables)
- Limit saturated fats; prefer unsaturated fats
- Control portion sizes
- Regular meal timing (avoid skipping meals)

## Indian Diet Specific
- Replace white rice with brown rice or millets (jowar, bajra, ragi)
- Use mustard oil or groundnut oil instead of palm oil
- Include dal, rajma, chana for protein
- Limit sweets, fried snacks, and sugary beverages
- 2-3 servings of fresh fruits daily (avoid fruit juices)
